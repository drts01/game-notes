**General & Group Identity**

- **Formation & Joint Venture:** KATSEYE was formed through the survival competition reality show *The Debut: Dream Academy*, a global collaboration between South Korea's **HYBE** and America's **Geffen Records**.
- **Name Origin:** Named after the **cat's eye (chrysoberyl)** gemstone, which reflects different colors depending on the light angle—symbolizing the group's global diversity. **Lara** suggested changing the spelling from "Catseye" to "Katseye".
- **Fandom Name:** **EYEKONS**.
- **Official Lightstick:** Announced as the **EYELIGHTER**.
- **Base of Operations:** Although trained using the K-pop system, KATSEYE is an American global girl group based in **Los Angeles, California**.
- **Netflix Documentary:** Their formation journey is featured in the 2024 Netflix docuseries ***Pop Star Academy: KATSEYE***.

**Discography & Key Release Milestones**

- **Debut Single:** **"Debut"** (Released June 28, 2024). The music video was filmed in Medellín, Colombia.
- **Breakthrough Hit:** **"Touch"** (Released July 26, 2024), which went viral on TikTok for its signature point choreography. A official remix featured **Yeonjun of TOMORROW X TOGETHER**.
- **First EP:** ***SIS (Soft Is Strong)***, released August 16, 2024.
- **Second EP:** ***Beautiful Chaos*** (2025), featuring singles like *"Gnarly"* and *"Gabriela"*.
- **Third EP:** ***Wild*** (2026).
- **Soundtrack Appearances:** Recorded the track *"Flame"* for the soundtrack of *Jentry Chau vs. the Underworld*.
